Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know before joining.

Do I need a formal diagnosis to join? expand_more

No diagnosis needed. Most members join because they've been living with bloating, low energy, or digestive discomfort for years without a clear answer from their doctor. If you know something is off, even if nobody has given it a name, this program is built for you. A formal diagnosis like IBS, Crohn's, or SIBO is not a requirement to benefit from understanding how your gut works and what it needs.

How much time does this actually take each week? expand_more

Each live session runs approximately 60–90 minutes. Outside of that, most members spend 10–15 minutes a day applying the protocols, whether that's reading labels at the grocery store, adjusting one meal, or reviewing notes. This isn't a full-time job. It's designed around everyday life, including people with demanding schedules, families, and travel. If you can commit roughly 2 hours per week, you can do this.

What if I can't attend a live session? expand_more

Every session is recorded and made available in the community within 24 hours. You won't fall behind. That said, the live sessions are where questions get answered in the moment, walkthroughs happen on your actual products, and the group energy is something the recordings can't fully replicate. We encourage showing up live when you can, but the recordings ensure you never miss the content.

Do I need to buy expensive supplements or specialty foods? expand_more

No. The foundation of this program is whole food, things you can find at most grocery stores. We do cover supplements in Session 5 and give you a clear tiered framework covering what's foundational, what's situational, and what's a waste of money. Nothing is mandatory. Members who follow the food protocols alone see significant results. Supplements, if you choose to use them, are an addition, not the strategy.

I've tried everything. Why will this be different? expand_more

Fair. You have probably tried Whole30, AIP, low FODMAP, a handful of probiotics, and at least one nutritionist who gave you a list of foods and sent you on your way. Every one of those approaches focuses on symptoms, not the underlying system. Elimination diets tell you what to avoid but not why or for how long. Probiotics help but don't address what's damaging your gut in the first place. This program teaches you how your microbiome works, what breaks it down, and what rebuilds it. When you understand the system, the right changes become obvious. Most members who say they have tried everything have not tried understanding their gut. That is what this is.

Is this safe if I have a serious condition like Crohn's, IBS, or SIBO? expand_more

Kyle himself managed his Crohn's diagnosis through food and lifestyle changes and has been off all medication for six years, so this program was built with serious gut conditions in mind. That said, this is an educational program, not medical advice. If you're under active medical treatment, we recommend keeping your doctor in the loop. Many members with IBS, Crohn's, and SIBO have gone through the program alongside their medical care and found it highly complementary. Use good judgment and consult your physician if you have specific concerns.

Is this suitable for vegetarians or vegans? expand_more

Yes. The program covers principles that apply regardless of your dietary preference. Some of Kyle's top recommended foods for gut support include animal products (bone broth, raw dairy, pasture eggs), but the framework is adaptable. Plant-based members are given alternative approaches throughout. The core of the program, understanding what damages your gut, how to read labels, and how to structure your lifestyle, applies fully to vegetarians and vegans.

How is this different from just watching YouTube or reading a book? expand_more

YouTube gives you information. This gives you a system, accountability, and direct access to Kyle. You can ask questions about your specific situation, your products, your pantry, your symptoms, and get answers live. The community means you're surrounded by people doing the same work, which dramatically increases follow-through. And the capstone session builds your personal gut protocol, a printed one-page document tailored to you. No book or YouTube channel gives you that.

When does the program start and how do I access it? expand_more

After joining, you'll receive an email with your access link to our private Circle community within 24 hours. Start dates for the live cohort are communicated there. Sessions run weekly over 4 weeks, with all recordings and resources available immediately in your member portal. Spots are limited per cohort to maintain the quality of live interaction. Once the cohort is full, enrollment closes until the next round.
